## 📊 What it does
The **Interactive OED Simulator** lets you explore the statistical probability of educational and economic outcomes in Italy based on socioeconomic origin (ESCS), geographical region, and school track — all backed by ISTAT, Eurostat, INVALSI, INPS, and INAIL institutional data.

## 🔑 Key Findings
- Italy's education system costs an estimated **€259 billion/year** (13.5% of GDP) in systemic failures.
- The **NEET rate** (15–29 year olds outside employment, education, or training) stands at **~13–15%** — among the highest in the EU.
- **Grade retention (bocciatura)** for low-ESCS students in vocational schools reaches **18.4%** in Year 1 vs **1.5%** in Lyceums for high-ESCS students.
- Italy's **shadow economy** removes ~**€83 billion** in tax revenue annually.
- **Brain drain** costs Italy ~**€8.2 billion/year** in lost human capital investment.
