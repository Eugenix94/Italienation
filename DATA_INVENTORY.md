# Italienation Data Inventory & Architecture Guide

This document serves as the master structural guide and dataset directory for external researchers, peer reviewers, and data curators accessing this archive on **Zenodo**, **OSF (Open Science Framework)**, or **GitHub**.

## 📊 Key Curated Panels in `processed_data/`

All datasets in `processed_data/` are clean, tabular (`.csv` or `.json`), and ready for direct import into **Excel**, **R**, **Stata**, **Tableau**, or **Python**.

### 1. International Policy Benchmarking & Social Mobility
- **`global_policy_response_benchmark_template.csv`**: Cross-country benchmark panel (Italy vs. 12+ OECD/EU peers) linking VET structure, ALMP (Active Labor Market Programs), equity financing, and early failure prevention.
- **`global_policy_response_benchmark_schema.md`**: Comprehensive data dictionary and normalization rules for the global policy-response panel.
- **`global_italy_position_oecd_wb_latest.csv`**: Cross-sectional ranking of Italy across World Bank and OECD education, cost, and access metrics.
- **`global_he_cost_access_panel.csv`**: Multi-country longitudinal panel tracking higher education cost burdens and financial aid accessibility.

### 2. NEET & Macroeconomic Stagnation Models
- **`neet_regional_model_panel.csv`**: Comprehensive regional panel combining NEET rates, GDP per capita, Gini inequality, education spending, and productivity.
- **`neet_regional_risk_model_predictions.csv` & `coefficients.csv`**: Statistical model outputs estimating regional NEET risk factors and territorial vulnerability.
- **`neet_gender_year_panel.csv` & `neet_gender_gap_by_year.csv`**: Time-series tracking of the gender gap in NEET incidence across Italian regions and macro-areas.
- **`neet_covid_period_summary.csv`**: Longitudinal analysis capturing the pandemic impact on youth disengagement and educational transition.

### 3. School Outcomes, Repeaters (*Bocciature*), and Early Leaving
- **`istat_repeaters_upper_secondary_latest.csv`**: Upper-secondary grade repetition (*bocciati*) by region and track (*Liceo*, *Tecnico*, *Professionale*).
- **`istat_lower_secondary_indicators_latest.csv` & `exam_proxy_latest.csv`**: Lower-secondary (*Scuola Secondaria di Primo Grado*) graduation and exam outcome indicators.
- **`snv_esiti_school_year_proxy.csv`**: Proxy dataset of school outcome and admission rates derived from official national evaluation data.
- **`anpal_replacement_early_school_leavers.csv` & `neet_by_migration.csv`**: High-granularity youth disengagement and early school leaving indicators categorized by citizenship and demographic background.

### 4. The OSINT Pillars: Shadow Economy & Brain Drain ("Ghost" Demographics)
This repository goes beyond institutional state data by formalizing systemic blind spots:
- **`shiw_shadow_tutoring_costs.csv` & `shiw_household_budget_survey_education_module.csv`**: Banca d'Italia microdata mapping the "Shadow Economy" of private tutoring (*Ripetizioni*), proving the structural "pay-to-win" disparity in secondary education.
- **`svimez_istat_brain_drain_regional_migration_panel.csv`**: AIRE/ISTAT migration data tracking the *Fuga di Cervelli*. Proves that missing NEETs and top-performers are often economic migrants to Northern Europe, a hidden demographic variable in regional statistics.

### 5. Territorial Finance, Textbooks, and Household Burdens
- **`anac_pnrr_m4c1_school_tenders_and_execution_panel.csv` & `cdp_opencoesione_school_infrastructure_safety_panel.csv`**: Hyper-local PNRR (Recovery Fund) and cohesion tenders cross-referenced to municipal school safety.
- **`italy_school_household_cost_snapshot.csv` & `italy_household_burden_module.csv`**: Empirical micro-data assessing the direct financial burden of secondary education on Italian households.
- **`siope_school_expenditure_summary.csv` & `siope_expenditure_by_region_year.csv`**: Public financial flows and municipal/regional spending on school infrastructure and territorial access.
- **`italy_mur_tuition_benchmark_2024.csv` & `atenei_payment_support_panel_2023_2024.csv`**: Higher education tuition fee distribution and regional financial aid (*DSU/ERSU*) coverage.

### 5. HuggingFace OpenData (`diatribe00/italian-schools-opendata`) & Municipal Indicators
- **`hf_teachers_by_school_order_panel.csv`**: Complete national teacher workforce breakdown comparing titular vs. supplementary/precarious contracts across school orders (*Infanzia, Primaria, I Grado, II Grado*) and post type (*Normale vs. Sostegno*).
- **`hf_upper_sec_track_enrollment_panel.csv`**: Regional and macro-area student enrollment distributions across the tripartite secondary system (*Liceo, Istituto Tecnico, Istituto Professionale*).
- **`hf_evaluation_scores_by_area.csv`**: Aggregated institutional self-evaluation scores (*SNV / Scuola in Chiaro*) across Italian macro-areas linking school outcomes (*CODICECRITERIO 22*) to structural dropout risks.
- **`openpolis_neet_metropolitan_capitals.csv` & `openpolis_educational_poverty_regional.csv`**: Municipal and regional child/youth deprivation indicators linking NEET incidence to early childhood nursery (*Asili Nido*) infrastructure deficits.

## 🔬 Reproducibility & Pipeline Scripts

Researchers wishing to re-execute the ingestion and validation pipelines can use the modular scripts in `scripts/` or explore the master analytical capstone:
- **`notebooks/italy_thesis_capstone.ipynb`**: The master narrative-driven thesis notebook synthesizing all 5 analytical scales (International, National, Regional, Track/Teacher, and Municipal) and embedding publication-ready diagnostics.
- **`build_hf_capstone_panels.py`**: Ingests raw Parquet data from HuggingFace `diatribe00/italian-schools-opendata` into analytical CSV panels.
- **`fetch_openpolis_data.py` & `fetch_eurostat_social_scoreboard.py`**: Automated APIs/crawlers for municipal and peer EU datasets.
- **`build_global_he_cost_access_panel.py` & `build_neet_expanded_panel.py`**: Rebuilds core macro-financial and regional datasets.
