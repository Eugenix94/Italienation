# SIOPE 2014-2019 Data Resolution

The files for SIOPE school treasury flows for the years 2014 to 2019 were previously empty (header only). 
This is because the SIOPE+ system did not systematically cover school-level transactions ("scuole") prior to 2020. 
Before 2020, schools reported through different Ministry of Education (MIUR) channels, and their SIOPE records are incomplete or missing.

**Action Taken:** 
The empty CSV files for 2014-2019 have been deleted. 
Analysis pipelines should begin their SIOPE time series from the year **2020** onwards.
